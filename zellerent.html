How the Agent Works

Observe. Reason. Act. Report.

ZelleRent follows the agentic loop — continuously sensing your inbox, making decisions, and taking action on your behalf.

1

Observe — Connect Gmail

The agent securely monitors your Gmail for Zelle and Cash App payment notifications across one or more accounts, around the clock.

2

Learn — Know Your Tenants

You define your tenants once: name, unit, sender alias, rent amount. The agent builds a model of your portfolio and uses it to reason about every payment.

3

Reason — Match with Confidence

When a payment arrives, the agent matches sender, amount, and timing against your tenant profiles — and flags anything ambiguous for your review.

4

Act — Receipt Sent

The agent sends a branded receipt, updates your records, and archives the payment. Full autonomy, or require your approval — your choice.

Agent Capabilities

Intelligence Built for Rental Properties

ZelleRent isn't just an app — it's an AI agent that understands your portfolio and works it autonomously.

Agentic Payment Matching

The agent reads payment emails, identifies the sender, and reasons about which tenant and unit the payment belongs to — no manual linking.

Autonomous Receipt Delivery

The agent composes and sends branded receipts on your behalf — "Flatstone Rental via ZelleRent" — the moment a payment is confirmed.

Configurable Autonomy

Set the agent's authority per tenant: Auto-Acknowledge for full autonomy, manual approval for oversight, or Auto-Archive to track silently.

Rent Intelligence

The agent knows each tenant's expected rent and tracks rate changes over time — so it can flag short payments and keep perfect records automatically.

Multi-Account Awareness

The agent monitors multiple Gmail accounts simultaneously — covering every property, every unit, every tenant in one unified view.

Human-in-the-Loop

Unmatched payments surface for your review. You stay in control — the agent handles the routine, you decide the edge cases.
